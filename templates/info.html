Welcome to Minto

Minto is a simple personal finance workspace for recording everyday money, understanding your financial activity, generating reports, and keeping group-trip expenses organized.

Everything in one place

Minto is more than an expense list. Each part is designed around a different piece of your financial life.

₹

Income & expenses

Record money coming in and going out, add notes, choose categories, and keep your day-to-day transactions together.

01

Accounts & cards

Manage savings/bank accounts, cash, and credit cards separately so every transaction is connected to the right source.

02

Investments & lending

Track investment entries with investment categories and maintain lending records with the person or counterparty involved.

03

Transfers made correctly

Credit-card bill payments and cash withdrawals are recorded as linked transfer entries so the movement of money is not mistaken for new income or spending.

04

Dashboard overview

Review expenses by category, spending by account, recent transactions, and the financial activity for the period you're viewing.

05

Reports & PDF export

Generate a date-range financial report as a PDF and keep a history of the reports you have generated.

Built for real-world tracking

  • Create your own expense and investment categories.
  • Track spending across multiple accounts.
  • Handle credit-card payments separately from purchases.
  • Record cash withdrawals without losing the transfer trail.
  • Review recent transactions from the dashboard.
  • Generate reports for a selected date range.
  • Use the app comfortably on desktop or mobile.
  • Install Minto as a PWA on supported devices.

Two modes, two purposes

Personal mode

Your everyday finances

Use Personal mode for your normal income, expenses, accounts, cards, investments, lending, dashboard, and financial reports.

Trip mode

Shared travel expenses

Create trips, add friends or participants, record shared expenses, define splits, and calculate who needs to pay whom at the end.

Your data stays scoped to your account.

Minto is designed around authenticated user accounts. Personal financial records are kept separate from other users, while Trip mode keeps each trip's participants and expenses within that trip.
