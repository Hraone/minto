Welcome to Minto

A simple personal finance app designed to help you record your spending, understand where your money goes, and keep your accounts organized.

01

Track every expense

Add income and expenses as you make them. Keep your daily money activity in one place instead of relying on memory or scattered notes.

02

Organize your accounts

Create accounts such as Bank, Cash, or Card so your transactions stay organized.

03

Understand your money

Use Overview to review spending, income, balances, and your overall financial picture for the selected period.

04

Keep card bills clear

Record card payments separately so transfers and actual spending don't get mixed together in your financial records.

Your data, your account.

Minto is built around your own account and your own financial records. Review your entries regularly and use accurate amounts and accounts to keep your Overview meaningful.
