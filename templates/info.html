Welcome to Minto

Minto is a simple personal finance workspace for tracking everyday money, planning the month ahead, staying on top of commitments, understanding credit cards, and keeping your long-term wealth in view.

Simple by design Built for everyday use INR friendly DD-MON-YYYY dates

Everything in one place

Minto is more than an expense list. Each part is designed around a different piece of your financial life.

01

Income & expenses

Record money in and out, add notes, choose categories, and keep everyday transactions connected to the right account.

02

Accounts & transfers

Manage savings, cash and cards separately. Transfers stay transfers, so moving money does not become fake income or spending.

03

Monthly Money Plan

Add your monthly salary and salary day to see where you are in the pay cycle and plan around the next payday.

04

Budgets

Set a monthly limit for categories such as food, travel, shopping or rent and see usage before the month runs away.

05

Safe to Spend

See what is realistically available after the unpaid commitments that still need to be covered this month.

06

Upcoming payments

Keep due-soon fixed expenses, investments and card bills visible so important payments are easier to remember.

07

Credit cards & EMI

Track limits, available credit, utilization, billing cycles and card-linked EMI or loan balances in one place.

08

Investments & Net Worth

Track investments, lending and your broader assets and liabilities, with a protected Net Worth view and historical snapshots.

Built for real-world tracking

  • Create custom expense and investment categories.
  • Track money across savings, cash and credit cards.
  • Keep transfers separate from actual income and spending.
  • Plan your month around salary day and recurring commitments.
  • Set category budgets and see current-month progress.
  • See Safe to Spend after unpaid current-month commitments.
  • Track card limits, utilization, billing cycles and EMI balances.
  • Use protected Net Worth and investment tracking for the bigger picture.
  • Generate date-range PDF reports for your records.
  • Use Minto comfortably on desktop, mobile and as an installed PWA.

A simple money routine

Minto is designed to make the useful financial habits easy to repeat.

1. Record Add income, expenses, transfers and investments as they happen.
2. Plan Set budgets, review upcoming commitments and know what is safe to spend.
3. Review Check cards, Net Worth and reports to understand where your money is going.

Two modes, two purposes

Personal mode

Your everyday finances

Use Personal mode for your normal income, expenses, accounts, cards, investments, lending, dashboard, and financial reports.

Trip mode

Shared travel expenses

Create trips, add friends or participants, record shared expenses, define splits, and calculate who needs to pay whom at the end.

Your data stays scoped to your account.

Minto is designed around authenticated user accounts. Personal financial records are kept separate from other users, while Trip mode keeps each trip's participants and expenses within that trip. Protected Net Worth has an additional unlock step, and biometric login can be enabled on supported devices.
